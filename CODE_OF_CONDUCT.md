# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful to people with different backgrounds in imaging, medicine, engineering, and research. Participate respectfully regardless of experience, language, identity, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give constructive, specific feedback and acknowledge uncertainty or mistakes.
- Respect contributors' time, privacy, attribution, and the limits of the project's evidence.
- Keep patient information and other private material out of public discussions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Do not pressure others to disclose confidential data or endorse unsupported clinical claims.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content or contact maintainers through an available private channel. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, ask participants to change their behavior, or restrict participation when needed. Reports should be handled with care, with personal information shared only as needed to address the situation. Decisions should consider context and apply the same standards to all participants.

This project-specific policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.
