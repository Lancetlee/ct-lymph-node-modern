# Attribution and redistribution scope

This is an independent research engineering project inspired by the workflow and audit of [dbouget/ct_mediastinal_structures_segmentation](https://github.com/dbouget/ct_mediastinal_structures_segmentation), Copyright (c) 2021, dbouget. Its BSD 2-Clause notice and disclaimer are preserved in LICENSE. This is not an official upstream release.

The new PyTorch Attention U-Net, checkpoint format, training and inference pipeline are not a faithful conversion of the old TensorFlow/Keras architecture. No legacy weights were downloaded, converted or redistributed, and original paper accuracy has not been reproduced. Upstream reference: Bouget et al., “Mediastinal lymph nodes segmentation using 3D convolutional neural network ensembles and anatomical priors guiding”, [doi:10.1080/21681163.2022.2043778](https://doi.org/10.1080/21681163.2022.2043778).

New project code and original documentation are distributed under the repository's BSD 2-Clause license. Dependencies, external publications, datasets and model weights retain their own licenses; links and citations do not grant redistribution rights. No clinical images, trained clinical checkpoints, private teaching videos, keyframes, transcripts or private source notes are included in this public repository.

The reusable skill contains general research and annotation guidance with links to public sources. It does not include a validated metastasis classifier, grant clinical approval or replace case-specific evidence. Synthetic execution tests do not establish clinical accuracy or M5 hardware performance.
